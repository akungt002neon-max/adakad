Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this service collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with its Telegram bot, AI generation features, and TikTok account integration.

1. Information We Collect

  • Telegram identifiers and basic public profile information needed to operate the bot.
  • TikTok account data made available through official OAuth or API authorization flows, such as open_id, display name, and related account metadata.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens, expiration data, and technical identifiers needed to maintain authenticated sessions.
  • Prompts, commands, generated content metadata, and service logs needed for functionality, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service improvement.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and secure access to bot features.
  • To generate images and videos requested by users.
  • To connect, maintain, and manage TikTok account integrations.
  • To enable authorized content posting or publishing workflows where available.
  • To monitor reliability, detect misuse, and improve service performance.

3. Legal and Platform Compliance

We use information only as necessary to provide the service and to comply with applicable laws, platform policies, and legitimate security requirements.

4. Credentials and Security

We do not ask for or store TikTok passwords through the Telegram bot. Access tokens and related credentials are stored in a secured server-side environment and are used only for authorized service functions.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with infrastructure providers, platform APIs, or service processors only when necessary to operate the service, comply with law, enforce policies, or protect rights and security.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. User Choices

  • You may disconnect your TikTok account from the service.
  • You may stop using the Telegram bot at any time.
  • You may request deletion of stored account-linking data, subject to legal or operational retention needs.

8. Third-Party Services

This service integrates with third-party services including Telegram, TikTok, hosting providers, and AI infrastructure providers. Their separate terms and privacy policies may also apply.
